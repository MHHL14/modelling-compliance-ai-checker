## 1 Purpose

1.1 This policy sets the Bank's framework for preventing, detecting and reporting financial economic crime (FEC): money laundering, terrorist financing, breaches of sanctions regulations, and fraud committed against or through the Bank and its customers.

1.2 The policy implements the Dutch Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (Prevention) Act (Wwft), the Sanctions Act 1977 (Sanctiewet 1977) and the related DNB guidance, and prepares the Bank for Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (the Anti-Money Laundering Regulation, AMLR), which applies from 10 July 2027. For fraud prevention it implements the transaction monitoring requirements of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/389 (PSD2 RTS on strong customer authentication).

1.3 Models and rules used for FEC purposes are subject to the Model Risk Management Policy (MRM-POL-001) and the Financial Crime Model Standard (FEC-STD-600).

## 2 Scope and applicability

2.1 This policy applies to all entities, branches, business lines, products, services, delivery channels and customers of the Bank, and to all employees and third parties performing FEC-relevant activities on its behalf.

2.2 It covers the FEC control framework end to end: risk assessment, customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, fraud prevention and detection, reporting, record keeping and training.

## 3 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| SIRA | Systematic Integrity Risk Analysis: the Bank's business-wide assessment of its exposure to integrity risks, including money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions and fraud risks. |
| CDD / EDD | Customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence measures. |
| Customer Risk Rating (CRR) | The risk classification assigned to each customer (Low, Medium, High, Unacceptable) that determines the intensity of CDD. |
| Transaction monitoring (TM) | Post-event or real-time detection of potentially unusual or suspicious transactions and behaviour through scenarios, rules and models. |
| Unusual transaction | A transaction that meets an objective or subjective indicator under the Wwft, to be reported to FIU-the Netherlands. |
| FIU | Financial Intelligence Unit — the Netherlands (FIU-Nederland). |
| FEC model | A scenario set, rule set, scoring or machine learning model used for CRR, TM, screening or fraud detection. |

## 4 Risk-based approach and SIRA

4.1 The Bank shall apply a risk-based approach: the intensity of FEC controls shall be commensurate with the risks identified for customers, products, services, transactions, delivery channels and geographies.

4.2 Financial Crime Compliance shall perform the SIRA at least annually and upon material changes in products, customer base or the threat landscape. The SIRA shall identify inherent risks, assess the effectiveness of controls and determine residual risks against the risk appetite (Wwft Art. 2b; AMLR business-wide risk assessment).

4.3 The SIRA shall take into account the EU supranational risk assessment, the Dutch national risk assessments and relevant typologies published by FIU-Nederland and supervisory authorities.

4.4 The outcome of the SIRA shall drive the design of CRR, transaction monitoring and screening: each material risk scenario identified in the SIRA shall be mapped to one or more detection controls, and gaps shall be recorded with remediation plans.

## 5 Customer due diligence

5.1 The Bank shall perform CDD before entering into a business relationship or carrying out an occasional transaction above the applicable threshold, comprising at least: identification and verification of the customer; identification of the ultimate beneficial owner and reasonable measures to verify their identity; assessment of the purpose and intended nature of the relationship; and determination of whether the customer or beneficial owner is a politically exposed person (Wwft Art. 3).

5.2 Every customer shall be assigned a Customer Risk Rating at onboarding using the Bank's approved CRR methodology. The CRR methodology shall consider at least customer, product and service, delivery channel and geographic risk factors, and shall be governed as an FEC model under FEC-STD-600.

5.3 Enhanced due diligence shall be applied to High-risk customers, politically exposed persons, customers linked to high-risk third countries identified by the European Commission, and other situations of higher risk (Wwft Art. 8). EDD shall include additional information on the source of funds and source of wealth and senior management approval of the relationship.

5.4 The Bank shall not enter into or continue a relationship with a customer rated Unacceptable, or where CDD cannot be completed.

5.5 Customer information shall be kept up to date through periodic reviews — at least annually for High-risk, every three years for Medium-risk and every five years for Low-risk customers — and through event-driven reviews triggered by transaction monitoring, screening hits or material changes in the customer profile.

5.6 Manual overrides of a model-assigned CRR shall be justified, approved by a person independent of the relationship manager and monitored for frequency and direction.

## 6 Transaction monitoring

6.1 The Bank shall monitor transactions and customer behaviour throughout the business relationship to detect transactions that are unusual in relation to the customer's profile and risk (Wwft Art. 3(2)(d)).

6.2 Transaction monitoring shall cover all customers, accounts, products and payment channels in scope of the SIRA. Coverage shall be documented in a coverage matrix linking SIRA risks to scenarios, and uncovered populations shall be justified.

6.3 Scenarios, thresholds and alert-prioritisation models shall be designed, calibrated, tested and periodically tuned in accordance with FEC-STD-600.

6.4 Alerts shall be investigated by trained analysts. Level-1 review of an alert shall be completed within 30 calendar days of generation; investigations shall be documented to a standard that allows reconstruction of the decision.

6.5 Alerts shall not be suppressed or closed automatically by a model unless the auto-closure logic has been validated under FEC-STD-600 and approved by the Financial Economic Crime Committee, and a sample of auto-closed alerts is subject to quality assurance.

## 7 Sanctions screening

7.1 Customers, beneficial owners, authorised representatives and counterparties shall be screened against the applicable sanctions lists — at least the EU consolidated list, the Dutch national sanctions list and the United Nations lists — at onboarding, upon list updates and upon changes in customer data.

7.2 Payments shall be screened before execution. For instant credit transfers in euro, the Bank shall verify its customers against EU targeted financial sanctions lists at least daily and immediately after new designations, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/886.

7.3 Screening configurations (fuzzy-matching algorithms, thresholds and list selections) shall be tested at least annually for effectiveness and efficiency, and changes shall be approved by Financial Crime Compliance.

7.4 Confirmed sanctions hits shall result in the freezing of assets and reporting to DNB without delay.

## 8 Reporting to FIU-Nederland

8.1 Unusual transactions shall be reported to FIU-Nederland without delay once the unusual nature has become known (Wwft Art. 16). The internal target is reporting within fourteen calendar days of the conclusion that a transaction is unusual.

8.2 The Bank shall not disclose to the customer or third parties that a report has been or will be made (prohibition of tipping-off).

8.3 Reporting decisions, including decisions not to report after investigation, shall be documented and retained.

## 9 Fraud prevention

9.1 The Bank shall operate transaction monitoring mechanisms that enable detection of unauthorised or fraudulent payment transactions, taking into account at least the risk factors specified in Art. 2(2) of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/389, including compromised authentication elements, transaction amount, known fraud scenarios, signs of malware infection and abnormal use of the device or software.

9.2 Where the Bank applies the transaction-risk-analysis exemption from strong customer authentication (Art. 18 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/389), it shall monitor fraud rates per payment type quarterly and cease using the exemption when monitored fraud rates exceed the applicable reference fraud rates (Art. 20–21).

9.3 Fraud detection models shall be governed under FEC-STD-600 and MRM-POL-001. Customer-facing effects of fraud blocking, such as declined payments, shall be subject to documented customer contact and release procedures.

9.4 Fraud statistics shall be reported to DNB in accordance with the EBA Guidelines on fraud reporting under PSD2 (EBA/GL/2018/05).

## 10 Record keeping and data

10.1 CDD records, transaction records, alert investigations and reports shall be retained for five years after the end of the business relationship or the occasional transaction (Wwft Art. 33).

10.2 Data used for CRR, transaction monitoring, screening and fraud detection shall be complete and accurate. Data feeds shall be reconciled with source systems at least monthly, and data gaps shall be reported to Financial Crime Compliance.

## 11 Training

11.1 All employees shall complete FEC training at onboarding and annually thereafter; employees in FEC roles shall complete role-specific training (Wwft Art. 35).

## 12 Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Management Board | Accountable for the FEC framework; a designated board member is responsible for Wwft compliance. |
| Financial Economic Crime Committee | Approves the SIRA, FEC risk appetite, material FEC model changes and auto-closure logic. |
| Business lines (1LoD) | Perform CDD and first-line monitoring; own customer relationships. |
| FEC Analytics (1LoD) | Develop and maintain FEC models under FEC-STD-600. |
| Financial Crime Compliance (2LoD) | Owns this policy and the SIRA; oversees CDD, TM, screening and reporting. |
| Model Validation – Financial Crime (2LoD) | Independently validates FEC models. |
| Internal Audit (3LoD) | Assesses the effectiveness of the FEC framework. |

## 13 Exceptions and dispensations

13.1 Exceptions require approval by the Financial Economic Crime Committee, are time-bound to a maximum of six months and are recorded with compensating controls. No exception may be granted to statutory obligations.
