## 1 Purpose

1.1 These Terms of Reference establish the Model Risk Committee (MRC) and set out its mandate, composition, decision-making rules, approval authorities, escalation duties and the tracking of conditions attached to its decisions.

1.2 The MRC is the senior management committee through which the Management Board exercises oversight of model risk in accordance with the Model Risk Management Policy (MRM-POL-001), CRD Art. 74 and 85 and, for IRB rating systems, CRR Art. 189.

## 2 Scope and applicability

2.1 The MRC's mandate covers all models of the Bank and its subsidiaries within the scope of MRM-POL-001, including AI, ML and GenAI models and vendor models.

2.2 The MRC acts under authority delegated by the Management Board. Matters reserved to the Management Board, in particular the approval of the model risk appetite and the approval of material aspects of IRB rating and estimation processes under CRR Art. 189(1), are prepared by the MRC and submitted to the Management Board with the MRC's recommendation.

## 3 Definitions

Terms defined in MRM-POL-001 apply. In addition:
- **Condition**: an obligation attached to an MRC or Model Approval Panel decision that must be fulfilled by a set deadline for the decision to remain valid.
- **Conditions register**: the register maintained by the MRC secretariat that records every condition, its owner, deadline, status and closure evidence.
- **Model Approval Panel (MAP)**: the sub-committee of the MRC that approves Tier 2 models and changes under delegated authority.
- **Voting member**: a member entitled to vote on MRC decisions under section 4.

## 4 Composition and membership

4.1 The MRC is composed of the following voting members: the Chief Risk Officer (Chair); the Head of Model Risk Management (Vice-Chair); the Head of Model Validation; the Chief Financial Officer or a delegate at managing director level; the Head of Credit Risk; the Head of Market and ALM Risk; the Chief Data Officer; and the Head of Compliance.

4.2 The heads of the 1LoD modelling units (credit risk modelling, IFRS 9 modelling, market risk and ALM modelling, financial crime analytics, enterprise stress testing and AI/data science) are permanent non-voting members. They present their models and are consulted on all matters concerning their models.

4.3 Internal Audit attends as a permanent observer without voting rights. The MRC may invite other experts, including representatives of the Data Protection Officer and Legal, as non-voting participants.

4.4 Members may delegate their attendance only to a named deputy at the next management level who has been notified to the secretariat in advance. A deputy exercises the member's vote.

4.5 A member does not vote on a decision concerning a model that he or she owns, developed or uses as model owner, or on a finding raised against his or her own unit. The conflict is declared at the start of the meeting and recorded in the minutes.

## 5 Meetings, quorum and decision-making

5.1 The MRC meets at least monthly. The Chair or the Vice-Chair may convene an extraordinary meeting at any time; any voting member may request one.

5.2 The quorum is met when the Chair or the Vice-Chair and at least half of the voting members are present, including at least one member from Model Validation or Model Risk Management. Decisions on a Tier 1 model require the presence of the Head of Model Validation or the named deputy.

5.3 Meeting papers are distributed at least five business days before the meeting. A request for approval of a model or a material change is accompanied by the validation report, the validation opinion, the 1LoD self-assessment, the list of open findings and known limitations, and the proposed conditions.

5.4 The MRC seeks decisions by consensus. Where consensus cannot be reached, decisions are taken by simple majority of the voting members present; the Chair has the casting vote. The Chief Risk Officer may veto any decision on risk grounds; a veto is escalated in accordance with section 8.

5.5 Dissenting opinions are recorded in the minutes with their rationale.

5.6 Decisions may be taken by written procedure between meetings if no voting member objects within three business days. Decisions by written procedure are recorded at the next meeting.

5.7 The secretariat (Model Risk Management) issues draft minutes within ten business days of the meeting. The minutes record attendance, conflicts declared, decisions, conditions and actions, and are approved at the next meeting. Minutes and meeting papers are retained for at least ten years.

## 6 Approval authorities

6.1 The MRC approves:
(a) Tier 1 models for use (gate G3 of MRM-POL-001), including the conditions of use;
(b) material changes to Tier 1 models and every model change that requires supervisory permission, before the application is submitted to the ECB (MRM-STD-050);
(c) the temporary use of a model with a *Not fit for purpose* opinion, for at most six months (MRM-POL-001 §10.1);
(d) dispensations for Tier 1 models and dispensations from validation before first use (MRM-POL-001 §13.2);
(e) MRM standards and material amendments to them;
(f) the decommissioning of Tier 1 models;
(g) the annual validation plan and the annual model risk report before submission to the Management Board.

6.2 The MRC recommends to the Management Board the model risk appetite statement and, for IRB rating systems, the material aspects of the rating and estimation processes that require Management Board approval under CRR Art. 189(1).

6.3 The MRC delegates the approval of Tier 2 models and material changes to Tier 2 models to the Model Approval Panel, chaired by the Head of Model Risk Management with the Head of Model Validation and the head of the relevant 1LoD risk function as members. The MAP reports its decisions to the MRC at the next meeting. The MRC may revoke or call in any MAP decision.

6.4 Every approval decision states the approved scope of use, the validation opinion on which it is based, the open findings accepted at the time of approval, and any conditions with owners and deadlines.

## 7 Conditions tracking

7.1 Every condition attached to an MRC or MAP decision is entered in the conditions register within five business days of the decision, with a unique identifier, the model identifier, the condition text, the accountable owner and the deadline.

7.2 The condition owner submits closure evidence to the secretariat before the deadline. Closure of a condition that relates to a validation finding is confirmed by Model Validation; other conditions are closed by Model Risk Management.

7.3 The secretariat reports the status of all open conditions to every MRC meeting, highlighting conditions due within the next 30 days and overdue conditions.

7.4 An extension of a condition deadline may be granted once, by the body that set the condition, on the basis of a written request submitted before the deadline. A condition that is overdue by more than 90 days is escalated in accordance with section 8.3.

7.5 Where a condition is not met, the MRC decides whether the approval remains valid, whether the scope of use is restricted, whether compensating measures such as a post-model adjustment are required, or whether the approval is withdrawn.

## 8 Escalation

8.1 The Chair escalates to the Management Board without delay: any red breach of the model risk appetite; any veto of the Chief Risk Officer; and any material supervisory finding or obligation relating to models that cannot be met by the deadline set by the supervisor.

8.2 Unresolved disagreements between the 1LoD and Model Validation on a validation opinion or on the severity of a finding are escalated to the MRC, which decides after hearing both parties. The decision does not alter the independent validation opinion, which is recorded unchanged together with the MRC's decision.

8.3 High-severity findings and conditions overdue by more than 90 days are escalated to the Management Board with the remediation plan and the compensating measures in place.

8.4 The Chair reports to the Risk Committee of the Supervisory Board at least annually on model risk and the work of the MRC, and without delay on any matter escalated to the Management Board under section 8.1.

## 9 Reporting and information

9.1 The MRC receives, at least quarterly, the model risk report prepared by Model Risk Management in accordance with MRM-POL-001 §11.2.

9.2 The MRC receives the outcome of every Tier 1 validation and every *Not fit for purpose* opinion irrespective of tier.

9.3 The MRC performs an annual self-assessment of its effectiveness and reviews these Terms of Reference at least annually.

## 10 Roles and responsibilities

10.1 The Chair sets the agenda, ensures that decisions are taken on the basis of complete information, and represents the MRC towards the Management Board.

10.2 The secretariat, provided by Model Risk Management, prepares the agenda and papers, checks the completeness of approval requests against section 5.3, maintains the decision log and the conditions register, and drafts the minutes.

10.3 Model owners present their models, are responsible for the accuracy of submitted information and implement the decisions and conditions of the MRC.

## 11 Exceptions

11.1 Deviations from these Terms of Reference require the prior approval of the Chair and are recorded in the minutes. Deviations from the quorum rules in section 5.2 are not permitted.
